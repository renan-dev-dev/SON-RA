Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what information SONØRA (“the Service”) handles. The Service is operated by Renan Felizardo.

1. Information the Service accesses

Only after you sign in with TikTok and approve the permissions, the Service receives:

  • Your TikTok open ID and basic profile information, such as display name and avatar, if you grant that permission.
  • An access token and a refresh token that allow the Service to post on your behalf.
  • The video files, captions and privacy settings that you choose to publish, and the status of each upload.

The Service does not collect your password, messages, contacts, location or payment details.

2. How the information is used

The information is used only to sign you in and to publish the content you asked to publish. It is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics.

3. Where it is stored

Tokens and settings are stored on the computer where the Service runs and are not sent to any other server, apart from TikTok’s own servers when needed to publish.

4. Sharing

Your information is not sold or shared with third parties. It is exchanged with TikTok only as necessary to authenticate you and publish your videos.

5. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until they expire or you revoke access. You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok account settings, in the section for connected apps and services. You can also ask for your data to be deleted by writing to the contact email shown at the bottom of this website.

7. Your rights

You can ask what information is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and withdraw your consent at any time, under the data protection laws that apply to you.

8. Security

Access tokens are kept private and are not published or shared. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but reasonable steps are taken to protect them.

9. Children

The Service is not directed to children and cannot be used by anyone below the minimum age required to have a TikTok account.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.

11. Contact

Questions or requests can be sent to the contact email shown at the bottom of this website.
