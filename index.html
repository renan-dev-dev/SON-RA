What is SONØRA?

SONØRA takes short videos that its owner has already made and uploads them to the owner’s TikTok account. It connects through TikTok’s official Login Kit and Content Posting API, so it only acts after the account owner signs in and approves access.

It is a tool for one creator publishing their own work. It is not a platform for other users.

SONØRA is built for creators who make their own videos and want to publish them to TikTok without uploading each one by hand.

Sign in with TikTok

The account owner approves the permissions the tool asks for. Nothing is posted before this step.

Choose a video

The tool takes a finished video and its caption from the owner’s computer.

Publish

The video is uploaded through TikTok’s Content Posting API.

Check the result

The tool reports whether the upload worked.
